### 🔢 Lab 4: Basic JavaScript Programs & String Manipulation
- **Factors & Range Primes:** Programs to print factors of a number and find prime numbers within a given range.
- **Patterns & GCD:** Displaying custom text/symbol patterns and computing the Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) of two numbers.
- **Digit Separation & Exponentiation:** Taking a 2-digit number, separating its digits, and raising the first digit to the power of the second digit.
- **Number to Words:** Converting a 0–999 number from a popup box into words with proper error handling ("out of range", "not a number").
- **String Vowel Operations:** Finding the first and last occurrence of the vowel "a" in a given word and extracting the substring between them (e.g., `ajanta` $\rightarrow$ `jant`).
